PORTIA
PORTIA LABIATA · THE SPIDER THAT HUNTS SPIDERS

You are a few millimetres of patience. Watch what moves, feel what trembles in the silk, follow what smells. Most of it can kill you.

CONTROLS

MOVE

WASD walk — up bark, stone, under leaves
C toggle sneak
Shift toggle dash: snap, dart, freeze

release leap to the ring

hold Q pluck · R change signal

hold F raise front legs
H hide HUD · M sound
G this intro again

HOW TO HUNT

  1. READPoint at or look at a spider to see its state and how alert it is.
  2. STALKSneak with C. Come from behind or above. If it turns, freeze.
  3. STRIKEHold Space, target an enemy, then release to jump and attack.
ScytodesSpits glue at whatever is in front of it. Never approach from the front, unless it's carrying its egg sac.
BadumnaHides in a tube. Stand on its sheet, pluck with Q, try signals with R until it comes out. Move when the breeze gusts.
ArgiopeWaits at the hub of its orb web. Sneak along the silk or drop in from above. If it pumps the web, freeze.

A dark male dancing on a heart-shaped leaf? Go there and keep still.
